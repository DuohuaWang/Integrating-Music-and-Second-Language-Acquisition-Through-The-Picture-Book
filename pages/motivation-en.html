Motivation

The Very Hungry Caterpillar

My motivation for this project comes from my learning process and passion for music and language, and is also based on my practical teaching experience. I have observed a serious challenge: many young students abandon learning a language (usually English) and music at the very beginning stages, often because teachers or parents turn these subjects to stressful obligations rather than an enjoyable process. Accordingly, I would like to develop a project that lets children learn happily, by integrating language and music through picture books.
